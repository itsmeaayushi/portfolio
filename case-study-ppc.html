Designing a PPC landing page that lifted qualified leads by 30%

This campaign page needed to be fast, persuasive, and conversion-focused. I crafted a focused landing experience that reduced distractions and guided users toward a clear action.

Problem

The existing campaign funnel had too many entry points and unclear value messaging, which diluted conversions and increased bounce rates.

Challenges

  • Ineffective headline and hero copy.
  • Confusing trust and feature sections.
  • Poorly prioritized call-to-action journey.

What we fixed

  • Refined the hero message to focus on qualified outcomes.
  • Streamlined sections into a single decision-making sequence.
  • Enhanced trust signals and clear next steps.
Role

UI/UX Designer

Timeline

15 Days

Team involved

Design, Marketing

  • Clear value hierarchy
  • Fast conversion flow
  • Trust signals up front

Motion used with intent, not for effect

The campaign page had to move users toward action without distractions. We focused motion on purpose, keeping the experience clean while making the next step feel obvious.

Results

  • Drove qualified enquiries from OEM and industry partners.
  • Became the sales and leadership team’s go-to reference during global events and pitches.
  • Improved engagement and time-on-site across Capabilities, Quality, and Metlux compared to the old presence.
  • Strengthened recruitment — candidates now get a clearer picture of Sutsnax's disciplines and culture before applying.
  • The site went on to win Best Automotive Website at the Digital Dragon Awards 2026 — recognition for turning a fragmented rebrand into a cohesive digital ecosystem.
