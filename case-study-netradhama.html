Launching Phaco Festival with accessible-first UX and impact

Phaco Festival needed a landing page that felt premium, human, and easy to use. I designed an accessible festival hub for Netradhama that prioritized clarity, readability and conversion for global creative audiences.

Problem

The festival experience was lost in a generic events page. Users struggled to understand sessions, speakers, and the brand story in one flow.

Challenges

  • Weak information hierarchy for event schedules.
  • Poor legibility and low visual contrast on mobile.
  • Unclear path to registration and festival participation.

What we fixed

  • Introduced a clear sectional layout for performances, speakers and tickets.
  • Built accessible colour contrast and readable text scale.
  • Added focused CTA areas to increase registrations.
Role

UI/UX Designer

Timeline

20 Days

Team involved

Design, Content

  • Trust through accessibility
  • Clear content hierarchy
  • Conversion-led event flow

Trust Transfer, Not Just a Redesign

The event identity needed to feel premium and approachable at the same time. We reinforced brand trust with clear navigation, readable typography, and a calm visual rhythm that supports festival discovery.

Results

  • Drove qualified enquiries from OEM and industry partners.
  • Became the sales and leadership team’s go-to reference during global events and pitches.
  • Improved engagement and time-on-site across Capabilities, Quality, and Metlux compared to the old presence.
  • Strengthened recruitment — candidates now get a clearer picture of Sutsnax's disciplines and culture before applying.
  • The site went on to win Best Automotive Website at the Digital Dragon Awards 2026 — recognition for turning a fragmented rebrand into a cohesive digital ecosystem.
