Simplifying a SaaS payments module to reduce friction

The existing payments interface was cluttered and confusing. I redesigned the module as a single, streamlined view that makes payment tasks fast, visible and intuitive.

Problem

Users were forced to move between multiple screens to complete one payment. This caused decision fatigue and increased abandonment.

Challenges

  • Multiple entry points for the same payment task.
  • Lack of clarity around payment status and options.
  • High cognitive load from redundant controls.

What we fixed

  • Reorganized the module into a single task-focused page.
  • Surface key payment states and next steps clearly.
  • Reduced form complexity and action ambiguity.
Role

UI/UX Designer

Timeline

18 Days

Team involved

Design, Product

  • Single-task clarity
  • Instant status feedback
  • Reduced form complexity

From Catalog to Configurator

Our payment module needed to feel like a single coherent step instead of a series of disconnected screens. The redesign brought the full task into view and let users move forward with confidence.

Results

  • Drove qualified enquiries from OEM and industry partners.
  • Became the sales and leadership team’s go-to reference during global events and pitches.
  • Improved engagement and time-on-site across Capabilities, Quality, and Metlux compared to the old presence.
  • Strengthened recruitment — candidates now get a clearer picture of Sutsnax's disciplines and culture before applying.
  • The site went on to win Best Automotive Website at the Digital Dragon Awards 2026 — recognition for turning a fragmented rebrand into a cohesive digital ecosystem.
