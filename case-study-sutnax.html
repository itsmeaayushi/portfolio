Redesigning Sutnax for speed, SEO & conversion

Sutnax was rebranded from Aspireli with a complex OEM story. The old site felt like a product brochure rather than a digital partner for automotive brands. I led the redesign to deliver clarity, performance and a confident brand narrative.

Problem

Aspireli was rebranding to Sutsnax, but the old website still looked and read like a basic supplier page. It had a plain logo, a generic "our customers" grid, and one paragraph explaining a business that actually spans OEM parts, printed electronics, and R&D. There was no way for a buyer to actually see the products, no separate space for their Metlux brand, and nothing about the site said "engineering-led innovation partner." The brand had outgrown its website.

Root causes

  • No real information architecture, just static pages.
  • No way to visually experience the products.
  • Metlux buried inside the main brand, no identity.

What we fixed

  • Rebuilt IA around how OEM buyers actually decide.
  • Added a 3D configurator to show products live.
  • Gave Metlux its own microsite and voice.
Role

Trust Transfer, Not Just a Redesign

The old site undersold the brand — a plain logo grid quietly listed their clients. We flipped that: Rolls Royce, Ferrari, Bentley, Jaguar are now front and center, because that credibility was already earned, it just wasn't being spent. The new site had to look like a company these brands would trust, before a single word of copy is read.

From Catalog to Configurator

Buyers couldn't tell how a body kit or badge would actually look on a vehicle from a product photo. So instead of better photography, we solved the actual problem — a live 3D model buyers can rotate and customize themselves. It turns a sales conversation into something a buyer can do on their own.

Motion used with intent, not for effect

Old site was fully static. New site opens with a brand-story video and product-in-motion clips — but placed only where it earns its spot (hero, product proof), not scattered everywhere. Motion is used to demonstrate real product texture and finish (especially for Metlux), which static images physically can't do.

Results

  • Drove qualified enquiries from OEM and industry partners.
  • Became the sales and leadership team’s go-to reference during global events and pitches.
  • Improved engagement and time-on-site across Capabilities, Quality, and Metlux compared to the old presence.
  • Strengthened recruitment — candidates now get a clearer picture of Sutsnax's disciplines and culture before applying.
  • The site went on to win Best Automotive Website at the Digital Dragon Awards 2026 — recognition for turning a fragmented rebrand into a cohesive digital ecosystem.
